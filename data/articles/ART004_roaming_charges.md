# International Roaming — Charges, Activation, and Disputes

**Article ID:** ART004  
**Category:** Mobile / Roaming  
**Last Updated:** 2024-01-15  

---

## Overview

This article covers international roaming charges, how to activate roaming, roaming data packs, and how to handle roaming charge disputes.

---

## Roaming Availability by Plan

| Plan | Roaming Eligible | Roaming Included | Pay-per-use Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mobile Basic | Yes | No | ₹10/MB data, ₹5/min calls |
| Mobile Plus | Yes | No | ₹8/MB data, ₹4/min calls |
| Mobile Max | Yes | 1 GB included in 30+ countries | ₹5/MB data, ₹3/min calls |
| Bundle plans | Inherits mobile plan rules | — | — |

> **Pay-per-use rates can accumulate very quickly.** Customers should always activate a roaming pack before travelling.

---

## How to Activate Roaming

1. Ensure the account is active and in good standing (no overdue balance)
2. Go to account portal → Add-ons → International Roaming → Enable
3. Select a roaming pack if desired
4. Roaming activates within 15 minutes
5. Customer must restart their phone after activation

**Important:** Roaming is disabled by default on all plans to prevent unexpected charges.

## Handling Roaming Charge Disputes

### Step 1 — Verify if Roaming Was Active
- Check account portal: Add-ons → Roaming history
- Confirm dates roaming was active vs. dates customer was travelling

### Step 2 — Check if a Pack Was Active
- If a pack was active: charges above the pack allowance are at pay-per-use rates — valid charges
- If no pack was active: all data/calls billed at pay-per-use — likely a surprise to the customer

### Step 3 — Assess Goodwill Adjustment Eligibility

| Scenario | Action |
|---|---|
| Customer was unaware roaming was possible | Apply 25% goodwill adjustment, maximum ₹500 |
| Roaming activated without customer consent | Full reversal of charges |
| Customer travelled without activating pack | Offer post-travel roaming pack at discounted rate (50% off) |
| Charges exceed ₹3,000 in a single trip | Escalate to Billing team for review |

### Step 4 — Educate and Prevent Recurrence
Always explain:
- How to activate a pack before the next trip
- How to set a data usage alert (MySelf app → Alerts → Data)
- That they can temporarily disable roaming from the app

---

## Countries Where Roaming Is Not Available

Roaming is not available in North Korea, Cuba, Iran, Syria, and a small number of restricted territories. If a customer travelled to one of these countries, all data would have been blocked — charges should not appear.

---

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate to the Billing Team if:
- Roaming charges exceed ₹5,000 in a single cycle
- Customer alleges identity fraud (phone used in another country while customer was in India)
- Account shows roaming usage from two countries simultaneously (possible SIM cloning)
