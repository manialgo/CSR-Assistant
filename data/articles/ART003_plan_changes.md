## Plan Change Rules

### Upgrades
- Effective **immediately** on request
- Customer is charged prorated difference for remaining days in current cycle
- New speed/data limits apply within 2 hours of upgrade

### Downgrades
- Effective from the **next billing date** (not immediate)
- No prorated credit for current cycle
- Customer keeps current plan benefits until end of cycle

### Mid-Contract Changes
- We do not enforce minimum contract periods for residential customers
- Business accounts may have 12-month or 24-month contracts — check before processing

---

## How to Process a Plan Change

1. Confirm the customer's **current plan** and **desired plan**
2. Check account status — suspended accounts cannot change plans
3. Confirm the customer understands:
   - Effective date (immediate for upgrades, next bill date for downgrades)
   - New monthly cost
   - Any prorated charges on next bill
4. Process in the account portal under **Plan Management → Change Plan**
5. Send confirmation email to customer (automated on save)

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I trial a higher plan and downgrade if I don't like it?**  
A: Yes. Upgrade is immediate. If you downgrade within the same billing cycle, the downgrade takes effect next month — you keep the faster speed until then at no extra cost.

**Q: Will my router need replacing if I upgrade to 1000 Mbps?**  
A: Only if your current router does not support gigabit speeds. Check the router model in the account portal. If a replacement is needed, it will be dispatched within 3 business days at no extra cost.

**Q: Is there a discount for annual payment?**  
A: Annual pre-payment plans offer 10% discount. Available on request — flag to account team.

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate to the Plan Management team if:
- Customer wants a custom plan or pricing not in the standard catalogue
- Customer is on a legacy plan no longer offered — special migration process required
- Business account with contract implications
