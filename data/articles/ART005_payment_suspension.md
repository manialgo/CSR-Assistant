# Payment Methods, Failed Payments, and Account Suspension

**Article ID:** ART005  
**Category:** Billing / Payments  
**Last Updated:** 2024-01-15  

---

## Overview

This article covers accepted payment methods, what happens when a payment fails, the account suspension process, and how to restore a suspended account.

---

## Accepted Payment Methods

| Method | Available | Processing Time |
|---|---|---|
| UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) | Yes | Instant |
| Debit / Credit Card | Yes | Instant |
| Net Banking | Yes | Up to 2 hours |
| Auto-pay (Standing Instruction) | Yes | On due date |
| Cheque / DD | No | Not accepted |
| Cash | No | Not accepted |

Payments can be made via:
- **MySelf App** → Billing → Pay Now
- **Customer Portal** → Billing → Make Payment
- **IVR** → Call 1800-XXX-XXXX and follow prompts

## Failed Payment Process

### Why Payments Fail

- Insufficient funds in bank account
- Card expired or blocked
- UPI daily limit exceeded
- Net banking timeout
- Bank declined (fraud prevention)

### What Happens After a Failed Payment

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| Day 0 (Due date) | Payment attempt. If fails → SMS + email notification |
| Day 3 | Second automatic retry |
| Day 7 | Final reminder. Account flagged for review |
| Day 14 | Account suspended (services paused) |
| Day 30 | Account marked for cancellation review |
| Day 45 | Account cancelled if payment not received |

## Account Suspension

### What Suspension Means
- All internet and mobile services are **paused**
- Incoming calls remain active on mobile (outgoing blocked)
- The customer can still access the customer portal and make payment

### Restoring a Suspended Account

1. Customer pays the outstanding balance in full (or arranges a payment plan — see below)
2. Payment must clear before restoration begins
3. Services typically restore within **1–4 hours** after payment confirmation
4. If services do not restore within 4 hours after payment → escalate to Technical team

### Payment Plans (Instalment Arrangements)

Available to customers with 3+ months of good payment history who are experiencing temporary hardship:

- Maximum 3 monthly instalments for balances up to ₹5,000
- Must be set up before account is suspended (cannot be set up post-suspension)
- One payment plan allowed per 12-month period

To set up: Customer must call support (cannot be done online). Requires agreement to auto-pay.

| Scenario | Fee |
|---|---|
| First suspension in 12 months | No fee |
| Second suspension in 12 months | ₹99 reconnection fee |
| Third or more in 12 months | ₹199 reconnection fee + account review |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: I paid but my service is still not back — what do I do?**  
A: Confirm payment has cleared (check bank statement). If yes, contact support with payment confirmation. Technical team will manually trigger restoration.

**Q: Can I get an extension on my bill due date?**  
A: Due date extensions of up to 7 days are available once per 12-month period. Requires account to be in good standing. Cannot be granted after suspension.

**Q: Will suspension affect my credit score?**  
A: We do not report to credit bureaus. Suspension only affects your service with us.

---

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate to the Billing Team if:
- Customer claims to have paid but account is still suspended and payment does not appear in system
- Customer requests a payment plan post-suspension
- Balance exceeds ₹10,000 (collections process applies)
- Customer is on a medical dependency plan (priority reconnection required within 2 hours)
