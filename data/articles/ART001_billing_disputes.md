# Billing Disputes and Incorrect Charges

**Article ID:** ART001  
**Category:** Billing  
**Last Updated:** 2024-01-15  

---

## Overview

This article explains how to identify, investigate, and resolve billing disputes including duplicate charges, unexpected fees, incorrect plan amounts, and disputed usage charges.

---

## Common Billing Dispute Types

### 1. Duplicate Charges
A duplicate charge occurs when the same service or fee appears more than once in the same billing cycle.

**How to identify:**
- Compare line items in the current bill against the previous month's bill
- Look for two identical amounts with the same description on the same date

**Resolution steps:**
1. Pull up the customer's current and previous bill in the account portal
2. If a duplicate is confirmed, raise a credit note for the duplicate amount
3. Apply the credit to the next invoice automatically
4. Notify the customer by email within 2 business hours

### 2. Wrong Plan Amount Billed
The customer is being billed for a plan that does not match their current active plan.

**Resolution steps:**
1. Verify the active plan in the account system
2. Check if a plan change was made mid-cycle — if so, prorated billing applies
3. If no plan change occurred and the amount is wrong, escalate to Billing Team with account ID and both amounts
4. Issue a credit for the difference if the error is confirmed

### 3. Unexpected Add-on or Service Charge
Customer sees a charge for a feature or add-on they did not request.

**Resolution steps:**
1. Check the account's add-on history for authorisation records
2. If no customer authorisation exists, remove the add-on and reverse the charge
3. If authorisation exists but customer denies it, escalate to the Billing Disputes team

### 4. International / Roaming Charges Disputed
See Article ART004 (International Roaming Charges) for roaming-specific disputes.

## Prorated Billing Policy

When a plan changes mid-cycle:
- The customer is charged the old plan rate for days used, plus the new plan rate for remaining days
- Prorated credit or debit appears as a line item on the next bill labelled **"Plan Change Adjustment"**
- This is **not** a billing error

---

## Refund Policy

| Scenario | Refund Timeline |
|---|---|
| Duplicate charge confirmed | Credit applied within 24 hours |
| Wrong plan billed (confirmed) | Credit applied within 48 hours |
| Unauthorised add-on | Credit applied within 24 hours |
| Customer error (authorised) | No refund applicable |

---

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate to the Billing Team if:
- The disputed amount exceeds ₹2,000
- The customer has disputed the same charge more than twice
- Fraud is suspected (unauthorised account access)
- The account has more than 3 unresolved billing disputes in 6 months

---

## What to Tell the Customer

> *"I've reviewed your bill and can see [finding]. I will [action] and you'll receive a confirmation by email. The credit/correction will appear on your next bill dated [date]."*

Always provide a reference number and timeline. Never promise same-day cash refunds.
