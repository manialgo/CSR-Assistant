# Account Management — Profile, Security, and Contact Details

**Article ID:** ART006  
**Category:** Account Management  
**Last Updated:** 2024-01-15  

---

## Overview

This article covers common account management requests including updating contact details, changing account holder, password resets, SIM management, and account closure.

---

## Updating Contact Details

### Email Address
- Can be changed by the account holder only (security verification required)
- Process: Customer Portal → My Profile → Contact Details → Edit Email
- Verification link sent to **old** email — customer must click to confirm
- If customer no longer has access to old email: must verify by calling support with DOB + account ID

### Phone Number (Mobile Contact)
- Process: Customer Portal → My Profile → Contact Details → Edit Mobile
- OTP sent to the **existing** number to confirm
- If customer has lost the old number: identity verification required (see below)

### Address
- Service address: requires engineer assessment if broadband service is impacted
- Billing address: can be updated directly in portal, effective immediately

---

## Identity Verification Protocol

Used when customers cannot access their registered contact methods:

1. Account ID (required)
2. Full name on account (required)
3. Date of birth (required)
4. Any two of:
   - Last 4 digits of payment card on file
   - Exact amount of last payment
   - Security question answer (if set)

All 4 required fields must match before any account changes are made.

## SIM Card Management

### SIM Replacement
- Customer requests new SIM (lost, damaged, or upgrading to eSIM)
- Verify identity first
- New SIM dispatched within 2 business days (standard), same day available at store
- Old SIM is automatically deactivated when new SIM is activated

### eSIM Activation
- Supported on: iPhone XS and later, Android devices with eSIM support
- Process: Customer Portal → SIM Management → Switch to eSIM → Scan QR code
- Takes up to 30 minutes to activate

### SIM Lock / Blocking
- If customer reports phone stolen: block SIM immediately via Customer Portal → SIM Management → Block SIM
- Charges continue until SIM is blocked
- Once blocked, no calls/data can be made — service resumes when new SIM is activated

## Account Holder Change (Transfer of Ownership)

Required when account needs to transfer to a different person (marriage, death, business transfer):

1. Both parties must provide identity proof
2. Process cannot be done online — must visit a store or submit documents by post
3. Timeline: 5–10 business days

## Account Closure / Cancellation

### Process
1. Confirm the customer wants to cancel (retention offer may be appropriate — see below)
2. Check for minimum contract period (business accounts only)
3. Confirm final bill date and any outstanding balance
4. For mobile: offer to port number out — customer keeps their number
5. For broadband: arrange equipment return (router must be returned within 14 days)

### Retention Offers (Offer Before Processing Cancellation)
- If customer is leaving due to price: offer 10% loyalty discount for 6 months
- If customer is leaving due to service quality: escalate to Retention team before cancelling
- If customer is moving abroad: pause account for up to 6 months instead of cancelling

### Final Bill
- Final bill issued on next bill date after cancellation request
- Outstanding balance must be cleared before account is fully closed
- Equipment charges apply if router is not returned within 14 days: ₹2,500

---

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate to the Account Management team if:
- Death of account holder (sensitive case protocol)
- Customer reports account taken over by someone else (account hijacking / fraud)
- Cancellation with disputed final bill
