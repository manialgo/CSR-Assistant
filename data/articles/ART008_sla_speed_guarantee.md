# Speed Guarantee and Service Level Agreement (SLA) Policy

**Article ID:** ART008  
**Category:** Policies / SLA  
**Last Updated:** 2024-01-15  

---

## Overview

This article outlines our Speed Guarantee, service uptime SLAs, how customers can claim compensation, and what our obligations are when we fail to meet commitments.

---

## Speed Guarantee Policy

### Minimum Guaranteed Speeds

| Plan | Advertised Speed | Minimum Guaranteed (Wired) | Minimum Guaranteed (Wi-Fi) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fiber 100 | 100 Mbps | 50 Mbps | 30 Mbps |
| Fiber 500 | 500 Mbps | 250 Mbps | 150 Mbps |
| Fiber 1000 | 1000 Mbps | 500 Mbps | 300 Mbps |

**Wi-Fi speeds are not guaranteed** — they depend on router placement, interference, and device capability. Only wired Ethernet speeds are subject to the guarantee.

### How to Claim

1. Customer must run a speed test on a **wired connection** at [speedtest.nexusnow.com]
2. Test must be run at least 3 times across different times of day
3. All 3 tests must fall below the minimum guaranteed speed
4. Customer contacts support with test results (screenshots or test IDs)
5. Support logs a Speed Guarantee fault — engineer visit scheduled within 5 business days

### Remedies

| Duration Below Guarantee | Remedy |
|---|---|
| 1–5 days | Engineer visit + root cause fix |
| 6–10 days | 3 days bill credit + engineer visit |
| 11–20 days | 7 days bill credit + engineer visit |
| Over 20 days | Full month credit + engineer visit + option to exit contract without penalty |

## Service Uptime SLA

**Residential:** 99% uptime per calendar month  
**Business:** 99.5% uptime per calendar month (business accounts only)

**99% uptime** = maximum 7.2 hours downtime per month  
**99.5% uptime** = maximum 3.6 hours downtime per month

### Calculating Downtime Credit

Credit is calculated per day of confirmed outage:
- 1 day credit = (Monthly bill / 30)
- Applied automatically to next invoice when fault is marked resolved and outage duration confirmed

---

## Planned Maintenance

- Planned network maintenance: 24 hours advance notice via SMS and email
- Maintenance window: 2 AM – 6 AM local time (minimise customer impact)
- Planned maintenance downtime does **not** count toward SLA calculations
- Emergency maintenance: may occur without notice; does count toward SLA if over 4 hours

---

## What We Do Not Guarantee

- Wi-Fi speeds (as noted above)
- Speeds during customer-caused network congestion (e.g., 50 devices on one router)
- Service during events outside our control (natural disaster, power outage, civil emergency)
- Third-party service speed (Netflix, YouTube — speed test to nexusnow.com servers only)

---

## Ofcom / TRAI Regulatory Rights

Customers also have rights under TRAI regulations:
- Right to exit contract if service is consistently below guaranteed speed for 30 days
- Right to written explanation of any outage over 12 hours
- Right to formal complaint escalation if unresolved after 8 weeks

To file a formal regulatory complaint:
1. Contact support and request a **Formal Complaint Reference Number**
2. Allow 8 weeks for resolution
3. If unresolved: customer may refer to the Telecom Disputes Settlement and Appellate Tribunal (TDSAT)

---

## Escalation Criteria

Escalate to the Service Quality team if:
- Customer threatens regulatory complaint / TDSAT referral
- Speed has been below guarantee for over 20 days
- Customer is a business account with uptime SLA breach
- Multiple customers in the same area report speed issues (potential infrastructure fault)
